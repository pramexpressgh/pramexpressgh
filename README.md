### about

**Pram Express Ghana Ltd** is a technology-driven logistics operator headquartered in Accra, Ghana. Founded in 2024, we move what matters — urgent documents across town, containerised freight across oceans — with real-time visibility, transparent pricing, and a customer-first operation.

We are building the logistics backbone for Ghana and West Africa. One shipment. One tracking update. One satisfied customer at a time.

### currently operating

Our network is live across three freight modes and 44 delivery zones in Ghana, with cross-border routes into West Africa and international lanes to major global destinations.

- Air Freight — urgent, time-definite and cold-chain cargo to and from major hubs
- Surface Freight — FTL and LTL road freight across Ghana and into West Africa
- Sea Freight — FCL, LCL and specialised ocean cargo with full customs clearance
- Live Tracking — real-time status updates at every checkpoint from booking to delivery
- Customer Portal — booking, waybill management and proof of delivery (in development)
- Fleet Expansion — additional GPS-tracked vehicles scheduled for 2025
